# Third-party notices

## US-align

StructLens supports a bundled or custom US-align backend. The source tree ships
the citation and license metadata under `src/structlens/resources/usalign/`.
Release maintainers must verify the current upstream redistribution terms,
record the exact binary version and SHA-256 checksum, and include the upstream
copyright notice before adding a platform executable to a release artifact.

## Biopython, NumPy, openpyxl, Pillow, matplotlib

These dependencies remain separate from StructLens licensing. Their notices and
licenses are provided by their respective distributions.
